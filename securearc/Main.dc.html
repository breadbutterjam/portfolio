What we do

From assessment to operations, under one roof

01

Consulting

Know your risk before you spend. Current-state assessment, gap analysis, facility risk levels and protection design, benchmarking, security strategy, vendor analysis, proposals and bid support.

02

System integration

End-to-end bespoke solutions for surveillance, perimeter security and integrated platforms, designed to your requirements and built with proven current technology.

03

Implementation & operations

Keep systems working after go-live. We implement systems you have already procured and run or maintain installed systems, subject to a minimum order.

How we work

A clear path from risk to a working system

1. Assess
Review the current state, run a gap analysis and define the risk level of each facility.
2. Design
Define the right level of protection, shape the solution and benchmark it against best practice.
3. Deliver
Manage vendors, contracts and SLAs, then integrate and commission the system.
4. Operate
Run and maintain the installed system so it performs long after go-live.

About SecurArc

Led by an engineer who has delivered at city scale

SecurArc was founded by Dhruvesh Sheth, an M.Sc. graduate of Cranfield University with around a decade in technology consulting and system integration. He has worked on projects with government bodies across Asia and Africa, including large city surveillance programmes, and previously worked at L&T and EY.

He has also served on the CCTV Manufacturing Committee for the Government of India. Our mission is simple: easy, affordable security solutions, grounded in real technical expertise.
